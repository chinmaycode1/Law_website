About Advocate Sunil Sawargaonkar

Advocate Sunil Sharadrao Sawargaonkar is a distinguished senior criminal lawyer with an exemplary career spanning over 15 years in the legal profession. Specializing in criminal defense, he has built a reputation for providing aggressive yet strategic representation to clients facing serious criminal charges.

His practice focuses on defending individuals and organizations against a wide spectrum of criminal allegations, from white-collar crimes and economic offenses to serious criminal prosecutions. With deep expertise in criminal procedure, evidence law, and constitutional rights, Advocate Sawargaonkar has successfully handled complex cases in trial courts, high courts, and the Supreme Court.

Known for his meticulous case preparation, persuasive courtroom advocacy, and client-centered approach, he has secured favorable outcomes in over 500 cases, including acquittals, bail grants, and reduced sentences. His commitment to justice and unwavering dedication to protecting his clients' rights have earned him the trust and respect of both clients and peers in the legal community.

Practice Areas

Comprehensive legal expertise across all domains of criminal law

Criminal Defense

Comprehensive defense representation for all types of criminal charges, from misdemeanors to serious felonies. Expert handling of investigations, trials, and appeals with strategic defense planning.

White-Collar Crimes

Specialized defense in economic offenses, fraud, embezzlement, corruption, tax evasion, and financial crimes. Expert navigation of complex financial investigations and prosecutions.

Bail Matters

Swift and effective bail applications in trial courts, high courts, and Supreme Court. Expert in anticipatory bail, regular bail, and bail cancellation matters with proven success rates.

Appellate Advocacy

Skilled representation in criminal appeals before High Courts and Supreme Court. Expert in challenging convictions, seeking sentence reductions, and filing review petitions.

NDPS & Drug Offenses

Defense in cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. Experienced in handling possession, trafficking, and commercial quantity cases with technical defense strategies.

Investigation Defense

Legal representation during police investigations, interrogations, and custodial proceedings. Protection of rights during arrest, search, seizure, and pre-trial proceedings.
