Privacy Policy

We respect the confidentiality of visitors and prospective clients.

Information collected

When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, phone number, case type, and message you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your consultation request.

Google sign-in collects your name and email address through Google Identity Services. These details are stored to link your contact requests and consultation bookings to you.

Consultation booking stores your preferred date, time, consultation mode, and any case notes you choose to provide.

Storage and security

Contact submissions are stored in MongoDB for case-intake and follow-up purposes. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing

We do not sell or share contact information with third parties for marketing. Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate the website or respond to your request, or where required by law.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the consultation form on the main website.
